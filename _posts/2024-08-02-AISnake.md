# Comparative Analysis of AI Methods for Snake Game; Heuristic, Q-learning, DQN, and PER

## Introduction

The development of artificial intelligence (AI) has made significant strides, particularly in game playing. The Snake game, with its simple yet strategic gameplay, serves as an excellent platform for evaluating various AI approaches. This report compares four AI methods for playing Snake: Heuristic, Q-learning, Deep Q-Networks (DQN), and Prioritized Experience Replay (PER), highlighting their implementations and performance.

## Overview

The Snake game is a classic arcade game where the player controls a snake that grows in length as it consumes food. The objective is to guide the snake to eat food while avoiding collisions with walls and its own body. The game ends when the snake collides with a wall or itself. The Snake game is challenging due to its dynamic environment and the need for strategic decision-making.

## Methodology for Implementation

The AI methods were implemented using Python and the Pygame library for game development. Each AI agent was trained on a custom Snake game environment and evaluated based on its performance in terms of average score, survival time, and efficiency. The performance metrics were used to compare the effectiveness of the AI methods in playing the Snake game. I used the PyTorch library for implementing the deep learning models. The training process involved running multiple episodes of the game and updating the AI agent's policy based on the rewards received. The evaluation process involved testing the AI agent's performance on unseen game environments and measuring its ability to achieve high scores and avoid collisions. To see the metrics, I used the Plotly library to create interactive visualizations of the AI agents performance.

To ensure a fair comparison, the same hyperparameters and training settings were used for all AI methods.





## AI Methods

### Heuristic

#### Overview

The heuristic method uses predefined rules to guide the snake's movements. The heuristic approach is based on simple rules such as moving towards the food, avoiding collisions, and prioritizing survival. The heuristic method is straightforward to implement but lacks adaptability to changing environments.

### Q-learning

#### Overview

Q-learning is a model-free reinforcement learning algorithm that learns the optimal policy by estimating the action-value function. The Q-learning agent explores the state-action space and updates the Q-values based on rewards received. Q-learning is effective for learning optimal policies in discrete state-action spaces.

### Deep Q-Networks (DQN)

#### Overview

Deep Q-Networks (DQN) combine deep learning with Q-learning to approximate the action-value function. DQN uses a neural network to approximate the Q-values and update the network weights through backpropagation. DQN is effective for learning optimal policies in high-dimensional state-action spaces.

### Prioritized Experience Replay (PER)

#### Overview

Prioritized Experience Replay (PER) is an extension of DQN that prioritizes the replay of experiences based on their temporal-difference error. PER improves the learning efficiency of DQN by focusing on experiences that contribute the most to learning. PER enhances the exploration and exploitation trade-off in reinforcement learning.
